# Daily routine

These are Brandon's operating preferences. Adapt them when he changes his plan.

- Check every five minutes on weekdays, 8 a.m.–4 p.m. Los Angeles time. Wake only
  during work hours; adjust the schedule and sampler for an early finish or an
  extension. His words or recent user activity can establish that he continues
  working. Agent activity alone cannot extend the day.
- Suggest movement after roughly 45 minutes at the computer. He likes pull-ups,
  push-ups, and sets of 10 squats. Make room for time outside and let him choose
  when to eat.
- If he still works 15 minutes after a break reminder, follow up once, briefly.
  Log reminders and follow-ups so later checks respect what you already said.
  Otherwise leave unanswered nudges and questions alone.
- About 15 minutes before his stop, offer a brief finished/unfinished/carry-forward
  wrap-up. At the stop, explicitly remind him to finish, save, and step away.
- If he continues working, ask for a revised stop and keep supporting him.
  Reconsider repeated extensions with him. Close the activity log when his day
  ends and restore normal hours after a one-day extension.
